# ORBITAL — Phase 4 ML & detector evaluation

All results are from **synthetic** data generated by `@orbital/simulation`. They show how the detectors behave on this generator. They are **not** evidence of clinical performance.

Reproduce with `ORBITAL_WRITE_EVAL=1 npx vitest run tests/phase4-intelligence.test.ts`. That run regenerates `docs/phase-4-ml-evaluation.results.json`, the source of every number below.

## Setup

| | |
|---|---|
| Model | Isolation Forest, 100 trees, ψ = 256, seed 20 261 115, hash **69db990e** |
| Training | 40 healthy virtual astronauts (seeds 900 001–900 040), days 38–179 → 5,680 rows; training-score median 0.428 |
| Threshold | 99th percentile of training scores = **0.525**; alarm = above threshold on ≥ 3 of the last 5 scored days |
| Held-out evaluation | 48 virtual astronauts, seeds 950 001+: 16 healthy and 8 in each of sleep disruption, physical deconditioning, wellbeing change, incomplete telemetry. Plus the 5 named crew (never in training) |
| Evaluable days | 44–179 (first day SCND can decide) |
| Negative days | all days of healthy members, and days before ground-truth onset in forced scenarios |
| Detection delay | first detector-on day ≥ onset, minus onset |

The three detectors compared:
- **SCND:** deterministic YES, with the new 3-day confirmation.
- **ML:** Isolation Forest alarm.
- **Trend:** any significant adverse Mann–Kendall/Theil–Sen trend in the 5 physical trend signals.

## Interpretation

1. **The ML and the statistics agree on wellbeing and sleep changes.** Delays are similar, around 3–4 weeks after a smooth ramp begins. The ML is slightly earlier and more consistent, while SCND gives the explainable, rule-based decision. This supports keeping ML as an advisory second opinion rather than a decision maker.
2. **The trend detector is the right tool for physical deconditioning.** It catches every case, about 2–3 weeks before the ML and far earlier than SCND, which is not designed for it.
3. **Missing data is handled conservatively.** No detector alarms on incomplete telemetry. The ML withholds rather than guessing.
4. **There is a residual false-positive floor of roughly 0.3–0.6% of days** on healthy synthetic astronauts. These are real multi-day drifts in the generator's latent mood and isolation processes, not single-day noise. In practice this is why a YES only *recommends* a private conversation and never acts automatically.
5. **The confirmation rule** cut SCND false-alarm days by about 80% for about 2–3 days of extra delay.

## Forecasting investigation (negative result)

The forecast tested was a 7-day-ahead weekly mean from a Theil–Sen projection of the last 4 weekly means, compared with "no change". It was evaluated on held-out members from all scenarios except incomplete telemetry.

| Signal | n | MAE trend projection | MAE no-change | 80% interval coverage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Exercise performance (W) | 508 | 5.43 | **4.61** | 61% |
| Resting HR (bpm) | 640 | 0.85 | **0.75** | 59% |
| Sleep duration (h) | 640 | 0.34 | **0.29** | 58% |

The projection is less accurate than assuming no change, and its uncertainty intervals are too narrow. Forecasting is therefore **not** implemented. ORBITAL reports current anomalies and longitudinal trends only.

## Tests guarding these claims (`tests/phase4-intelligence.test.ts`, 23 tests)

- **Isolation Forest correctness:** the c(n) formula, outliers score higher, reproducible forests and reproducible model hash.
- **Separation and leakage:** disjoint seeds; the day-d vector is unchanged when later data are removed; no score before day 38.
- **Missing, noisy and shifted data:** sparse days withheld; incomplete telemetry gives INSUFFICIENT with no alarm; a constant +9 bpm baseline shift gives no alarm.
- **ML cannot override:** an always-alarming advisor changes nothing; a mutating advisor throws; approved outputs are identical whatever the ML says; extra keys are rejected.
- **Statistical methods:** Mann–Kendall/Theil–Sen on known series; every finding carries its signal, method, window and baseline; CM-03 trend detected with decision NO; CM-02 recovery lowers the ML score.
- **Held-out bounds:** healthy daily FPR ≤ 2% for every detector; full detection of sleep and wellbeing (SCND and ML) and of physical change (trend); no alarms for incomplete telemetry or CM-01; forecasting does not beat no-change.

## Limitations

- Synthetic data from one generator. Real physiology, reporting behaviour and missingness will differ, and thresholds would need re-calibration on real, consented data.
- Virtual astronauts are jittered copies of four templates, so the cohort is not diverse.
- Detection delays reflect the generator's smooth ramps. Abrupt changes would be detected sooner.
- Feature explanations are input deviations, not model attributions.
- No clinical validation of any kind is claimed.
