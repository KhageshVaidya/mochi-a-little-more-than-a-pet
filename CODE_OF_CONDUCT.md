# Code of Conduct

Mochi is about looking after people. We expect the same of everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and welcoming, especially to newcomers.
- Respecting different viewpoints, experiences and levels of expertise.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Treating health, wellbeing and privacy topics with care.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including health information, without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption, trolling or personal attacks.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities and enforcement

Project maintainers clarify these standards and take fair, appropriate action when they are not met, including removing comments or contributions, or temporarily or permanently banning a participant.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers using GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (Security tab → *Report a vulnerability*), marking the report as a conduct issue, or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
